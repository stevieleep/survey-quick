Survey Quick Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 10, 2026

Survey Quick is designed to help users create surveys, record observations, review results, and export data.

Data Storage

Survey data is stored locally on the user's device.

Survey Quick does not automatically upload survey data to developer-operated servers.

Personal Information

Survey Quick does not require user accounts.

Survey Quick does not collect names, addresses, phone numbers, or other personal information.

In-App Purchases

Survey Quick offers an optional one-time purchase called Survey Quick Premium.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.

CSV Exports

Survey Quick creates CSV files only when requested by the user.

Users control where exported files are saved or shared.
