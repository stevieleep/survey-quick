Frequently Asked Questions

How many categories can a survey contain?

The free version supports up to 4 categories per survey.

Survey Quick Premium increases the limit to 15 categories.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. Survey Quick Premium is a one-time purchase.

How do I restore purchases?

Open Settings inside Survey Quick and tap Restore Purchases.

Where is my survey data stored?

Survey data is stored locally on your device.

How do I export data?

Open a survey, view the data table, and choose the CSV export option.
